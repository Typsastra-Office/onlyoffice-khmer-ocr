# Dictionary data notice

`data/khmer_dictionary.kdict` is a compiled derivative of Seanghay Hay's
[Khmer Dictionary 44k](https://huggingface.co/datasets/seanghay/khmer-dictionary-44k).

The author has confirmed that the dictionary may be redistributed for
non-commercial use with attribution. Credit must remain visible when this demo
or its compiled KDIC is redistributed. This permission applies to the
dictionary data, not automatically to unrelated third-party material.

The frequency weights encoded in KDIC were generated by the Khmer Segmenter
project from segmentation results. The application and Rust/WASM source code
remain subject to their repository software license.

The RAC layer also contains manually reviewed adaptations mined from RAC
headword phrases, definitions, and examples. These entries retain RAC and
Seanghay Hay attribution in the embedded KDIC provenance; occurrence in RAC
prose was reviewed and was not treated as automatic spelling authority.

The local inclusive build overlays separately reviewed geography, science,
economics, law, and digital-terminology lexicons. Their source identities and
per-word evidence are embedded in KDIC. This notice does not grant permission
to redistribute those independent source publications; review each source's
terms before publishing the combined binary.

The local v0.2 inclusive build also contains an AI-assisted, segmentation-only
pilot derived from frequency and contextual observations in
[Panhapich's Khmer Text Corpus](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Panhapich/khmer-text-corpus).
The dataset card labels its license as `other` and notes inherited source terms.
This attribution records provenance and is not an independent license grant.
